# Design pillars

These are design constraints. When a system decision contradicts one of them,
the pillar wins unless the operator changes it.

1. **Pehlichi repairs, the player enables.** The glitch loop is a partnership.
   The player's verbs are explore, notice, command, protect, gather and clear.
   See [GLITCH-AND-PEHLICHI.md](GLITCH-AND-PEHLICHI.md).
2. **Low-attention play is a first-class mode.** Players should be able to
   spend long stretches salvaging, crafting, repairing, building, organizing,
   exploring stabilized areas and improving a home, with no mandatory combat
   interrupting them.
3. **Danger is a gradient away from home.** Risk rises with distance from
   home and stabilized areas. Later, the nefarious AI may actively send hunters
   after the player out there.
4. **Major danger is player-initiated or geographically telegraphed.** Random
   mandatory base raids are *not* a core requirement.
5. **Building in dangerous places is a deliberate risk/reward choice.**
   Building/stabilization will eventually suppress hostile spawning within a
   local radius, similar in principle to Valheim. The radius is data, and the
   system is not built during bootstrap.
6. **Normal things look physical; glitched things reveal the Grid.** See
   [VISUAL-DIRECTION.md](VISUAL-DIRECTION.md).
7. **Organic building.** Construction snaps via sockets (Valheim-like), not a
   strict grid, even though the world's aesthetic is grids.
