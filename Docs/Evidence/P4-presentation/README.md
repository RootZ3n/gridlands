## Subtitle and dialogue layer (`UGLSubtitleSubsystem`, `FGLSubtitleQueue`, HUD)

**The director still owns what is said and when.** Queueing, story priority, deferral, cooldowns
and repetition protection are unchanged. The subtitle layer only presents the director's line
stream (`OnLine`). A new exchange starting mid-another means the director allowed an
interruption, so the earlier exchange's unplayed lines are dropped.

**Speakers are distinguished by name, not only colour.** "NICE:" in magenta and "PEHLICHI:" in
cyan, white text on a 62% black box, wrapped to 60% of the screen width, with at most 2 lines at
once.

**Accessibility.**
- `gl.Subtitles 0/1` turns subtitles on or off.
- `gl.Subtitles.Scale` changes reading time.
- `gl.Subtitles.TextScale` changes text size.
- Duration is reading time (about 2.6 words a second, 2–9 s), times the scale.

**Voice hook.** An exchange line may name a `voice` sound asset. The subtitle layer then plays it
at the line's start and uses its length as the subtitle's duration. No voice assets are needed
now.

**Developer text.** The debug on-screen text is off by default; `bShowOnScreen` is the fallback.

## Interaction prompt
The prompt comes from the focused interactable's options:
- enabled: "[E] Salvage Fence panel";
- disabled, greyed with the reason: "Place something here (Nothing you carry fits here yet)".

It is hidden when nothing is in reach or a build or terraform tool is out, so it never clutters.

## Visual proofs (temporary; not final art)
**Terrain.** `M_TerrainVertexColor` is generated by `Tools/build-blockout.sh`. The chunk computes
vertex colours from slope (heightfield gradient) and from edits (height against the authored
base). There is no texture work.

**De-rez.** `UGLDerezComponent` and `GLDerez::Apply` are one dissolve shared by creatures and
storm artifacts:
- gameplay state changes first (defeated, collision off);
- the look follows over 1.2 s;
- a defeat restored from a save is already gone, with no replayed dissolve.
